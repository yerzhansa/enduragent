User-facing: The app now uses one FTP, the one in intervals.icu, shows each ride's FTP and the FTP history on Activity review, and sets a coach's proposed FTP in intervals.icu only after you confirm. When intervals.icu has no FTP the coach has none either, while a new Plan still starts from intervals.icu's estimate, or the FTP you typed, until you set one there. Chat's Change one thing no longer offers Correct FTP; past FTP changes stay in Plan history, and Plan details no longer shows the FTP guidance they wrote.
