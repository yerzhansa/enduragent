Chat shows the spend cap once under Stop. When the Stop banner is up, a refused queued message or a refused decision answer no longer adds a second copy of the cap message under it; the refusal still shows when the banner is not up yet, and on the Plan surface, which has no banner. This refines the unreleased Stop mode, so it adds no release note of its own.
