## notes

- On rolling endurance rides, hold a steady endurance effort on climbs rather than surging; use conversational breathing and comfortable cadence through the middle hours (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- For rides over two hours: drink one bottle per hour, eat a small amount every 30 minutes, and have a slightly larger breakfast when sustained climbing is planned; rehearse this routine on easy rides first (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- In headwinds on exposed sections, tuck lower on the bars and move snack timing earlier to stabilize energy in the final hour (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
