## notes
_updated: 1998-07-06
- On rolling endurance rides, hold a steady endurance effort on climbs rather than surging, with conversational breathing and comfortable cadence through the middle hours (coach/athlete, 1998-07-06).
- For rides over two hours: target one bottle per hour and a bite of food every 30 minutes; eat a slightly larger breakfast when sustained climbing is planned (coach/athlete, 1998-07-06).
- Practice the fueling routine on easy rides before using it on longer weekend rides (coach/athlete, 1998-07-06).
- In wind on exposed river sections, use a lower position on the bars and move snacks earlier to stabilize energy in the final hour (coach/athlete, 1998-07-06).
