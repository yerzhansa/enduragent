## notes
_updated: 1998-07-06
- On rolling endurance rides, hold a steady endurance effort on climbs rather than surging; keep breathing conversational and cadence comfortable through the middle hours (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- In headwinds on exposed stretches, tuck lower on the bars and move snacks earlier to stabilize energy in the final hour (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- For rides over 2 hours: target one bottle per hour, eat something every 30 minutes, and have a slightly larger breakfast when sustained climbing is planned (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- Practice the fueling routine on easy rides before relying on it during longer weekend rides (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).

## cycling-history
_updated: 1998-07-06
- On the second day of back-to-back rides, mild leg heaviness has cleared after about 20 minutes of easy spinning, considered a normal response during the current build (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- Current adjustment rule: if second-day leg heaviness lasts beyond the first 30 minutes, shorten that ride rather than pushing through (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
- Monitor hand and shoulder comfort after longer periods in a lower bar position, especially as weekly riding volume increases (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06).
